# Know Thyself Diary

Build a focused tool that solves the problem described below.

MAKE IT YOURS 
Who this tool is for:  This tool is for a spiritual individual who experiences chronic pain.   They are tired of apps that feel cold and compartintalized. They want to take control of their life in a holistic and analytical matter. They care deeply about themselves and the environment and believe spiritual health is just as important as emotional, mental, and physical. They seek to Know Thyself.  
The task it should help with:  It helps to track pain and other inputs the body mind and spirit has experienced in a day.   Over time it shares an overview of areas that might need attention. Think of it like a diary that incorporates the body.   Over time, it could give suggestions like to excercise a certain body part, drink more water if there are headaches or dry mouth, or seek a massage or the like. These suggestions might also be guided by the phase of the moon and what sidereal astrologicial signs the moon and sun are in.  
How this works today:  They currently have notes about their phsyical and mental health spread across journals, notesapp, health apps, google calendars, and old doctor visits. When they go to see a doctor they go through their notes to piece together what has been happening and lose track of what pains are normal or new.  
What goes wrong:  They might be missing patterns that arise around their period. A certain side of the body that gets injured more. Or reocurring symptoms they forget between doctor visits. This would have a visual element that ties to astrology and other spiritual systems in the future.  
Who submits requests:  Human experiencer  
Information each request needs:  date, part of body, notes on area  
Workflow stages:  Ready > Add > Updated 
What success looks like:  The experiencer sees an overview of a human body with subtle gradient “auras” over areas they have listed or made note of. Over time areas that experience a lot of notes might glow more intensly and show a need for attention. 

They can click on the human body outline and a box will appear for them to input notes on what they are feeling and edit the date if needed to record something previously. They might be able to attach images too. For example: They click on a part of the body > a dialouge pops up > They input information about a fall and attach an image of a bruise > They might note they drank the night before > They close the box > a faint aura shows over that area. They might click to update more pain at another time. Over time the area may glow brighly and give recommendations like taking a salt bath, PT excerceises, or to visit a doctor.  
Colors and style:  off white beige as a base like aged paper. The background should have a subtle paperlike texture for some grit. The vibe is esoteric and ancient wisdom mixed with solar punk. When I think of solar punk I think of greens, beiges, a sort of hopeful vibrancy or holographic sheen in subtle places. The black color used should be off black with a purplish hue. The font should be a mix of serif and sans-serif.   Accessibility should be key. IT should be easily readible by a screen reader and have good font size and contrast. It should be able to switch between english and mexican spanish.  
Use plain language, large mobile-friendly inputs, and clear confirmation after submission.   This has examples to pull from: https://www.pinterest.com/fulsomeswitch/esoteric/app/
